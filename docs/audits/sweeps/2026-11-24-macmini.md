## Method

Preview visible requested but reported false. For /app, three full navigation:none loads at 1280 with normal cache; a passive MutationObserver on document captured appearance of main empty-state action /app/people/new using performance.now(). FCP entries absent after an additional 1.2 seconds. Resource figures are medians at that point, exclude document and are limited by caching and cross-origin visibility. Other routes lack usable three-sample timings. No post-load estimates substituted.

At 390 px the iphone-12-pro preset and `window.innerWidth` both confirmed the viewport. Normal cache was not cleared. Console and network logs were cumulative and capped, so per-route error counts cannot be attributed. API endpoints, submissions and mutations were intentionally excluded. Menu reachability and dead ends remain incompletely assessed.
