## How the project is shaped

Signed-in pages live under `app/app/*` (including canonical `app/app/people/[personId]/{ai,raw,contextualized}` with 1-line compatibility re-exports in `app/app/source-docs/[personId]/*`); public routes (`/`, `/stories/[id]`, `/ai.txt`, `/updates`) sit beside them. Live UI lives in `components/` (`vault/`, `queue/`, `ai/`, `marketing/`, six live primitives in `components/design/`); frozen before-snapshots are isolated in `components/design-preview/`. `extension/` holds the Chrome capture extension, `scripts/` holds the 58-step `pnpm verify` suite, `docs/tracker/` is the Card tracker, and `docs/planning/site-guide.md` is product truth.
