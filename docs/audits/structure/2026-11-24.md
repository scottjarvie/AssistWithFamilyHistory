## 1. Verdict

The repo is a moderate distance from "easy for a future AI to work in": about two weeks of short, careful threads, not a rebuild. Most of it is already in good order. The site's backend has four files of 2,200 to 2,800 lines, and three of them do five or six unrelated jobs each. The three biggest reasons:

1. **The rules for saving a person live in five live places** (plus two dead copies). The website, the import, the first-start flow, provisional relatives and the AI connector each insert people themselves. This morning's living-relative bug (`F15`) was in one of those copies. An agent fixing one copy cannot see the others.
2. **The privacy guards read code by file path and text.** 31 check scripts read source text, nine of them from the big files. Several assert that something is *absent* between two function names. If code moves out of the file, those checks keep passing while checking nothing. They must be hardened before anything is split.
3. **One idea, several names.** "Operations" means three things. "Connections" means two. There are two different queues. Nothing maps the names across the backend, the AI connector and the screens.

Most of the split work below is a pure move. But the code that decides living status, privacy and publishing sits inside the same big files. Under your rule, moving it is your call even when the move changes nothing. **One decision from you (N0 below) unlocks most of the remaining work.**
