# Public Story Privacy Sweep Checklist

Use this before enabling search indexing or broad beta sharing for published story pages.

## Story Status

- Confirm the story is `published`; draft and review pages must remain unavailable publicly.
- Confirm the public URL uses the readable slug and the legacy ID URL redirects to it.
- Confirm `publicIndexing` is `noindex` unless the story has been explicitly approved for indexing.
- Confirm the story has a publish-preview audit snapshot with blocker and warning counts.
- Confirm publisher notes explain why the story was approved.

## Evidence And Provenance

- Confirm the story has at least one source group visible in the provenance panel.
- Confirm the key life events used in the story are supported by citations or source records.
- Confirm context claims are tied to place, era, or locality reports.
- Confirm unresolved provisional relatives are either resolved or clearly excluded from public claims.

## Living And Private-Risk Review

- Confirm the anchor person is not marked living.
- Confirm close relatives with missing death dates have been reviewed.
- Confirm modern family edges, private notes, sensitive memories, and young-family situations are not exposed.
- Confirm any high-risk story has `secondReviewRequired` enabled and second approval marked complete.

## Share Surface

- Confirm Open Graph and metadata use only published story/person data.
- Confirm share descriptions do not expose private notes, unpublished review text, or internal audit details.
- Confirm no unpublished story URL renders outside the authenticated app.

## Production Data Sweep

- Sample recent imported FamilySearch Capture records for private notes and living-person indicators.
- Check public stories for accidental inclusion of internal agent comments or provisional research language.
- Verify takedown contact path is visible from public pages and privacy page.
- Record the sweep date, reviewer, second reviewer if needed, and final indexing decision in the story audit history.
