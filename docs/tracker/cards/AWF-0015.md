## Why this exists

Family research is often shared with a named relative or researcher who needs
ongoing account-bound access, attribution, and limited operations.

## Current truth

Private vault operations are single-owner scoped. No invite/accept lifecycle,
Viewer/Contributor/Editor/Owner role model, project/record scope, separate
interpretation model, operation matrix, attribution, revoke path, or
collaborator activity view is verified.

## Scope

- Invite a named Assist With Family History account to an explicit
  archive/project/share/record boundary.
- Use four plain roles: Viewer reads approved shared material; Contributor adds
  sources, notes, candidate facts, and hypotheses; Editor organizes/improves
  shared records; Owner manages the granted archive boundary, sharing, and
  permissions.
- Keep role grants explicit, scoped, reviewable, and revocable; Viewer never
  implies edit, Contributor never implies merge/publish, Editor never implies
  ownership, and Owner applies only within the granted boundary.
- Preserve shared evidence with separate interpretations, including each
  researcher's or authorized AI's rationale, confidence, sources, reasoning,
  authorship, and history. Do not force one official answer.
- Enforce living/private, AI-use, and publication boundaries per collaborator.
- Record acceptance, action attribution, scope change, and revoke.
- Keep Trusted separate from owner, Unlisted, and Public modes.

## Next safe action

Design the authorization matrix after owner isolation, data lifecycle, and
activity history are complete.

## Unknowns and access limits

Shared identity across Assist products is not available or implied. No real
relative or private project may be used for test evidence. Deceased status must
come from explicit assertion/evidence rather than age heuristics.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes invite/accept/decline/revoke scenarios, all four
roles, per-record and per-operation allow/deny fixtures, two collaborators
retaining different interpretations of shared evidence, attribution, living /
private filtering, separate publish denial, and two-identity browser proof.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Named collaborators receive only explicit records and operations.
- [ ] Viewer, Contributor, Editor, and Owner remain simple, scoped, explicit,
  and revocable without enterprise permission complexity.
- [ ] Shared evidence supports separate attributed hypotheses/conclusions
  without silent conflict resolution or canonical promotion.
- [ ] Invitation, acceptance, scope changes, and revocation are attributable.
- [ ] Read does not imply write, merge, publish, invite, or delete.
- [ ] Living/private and AI-use rules remain enforced per collaborator.
- [ ] Trusted, Unlisted, owner, and Public access remain independent.
- [ ] Security, schema, PR/CI/deployment, live proof, and independent audit exist.
