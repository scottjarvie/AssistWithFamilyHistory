## Why this exists

People need a practical way to take their family-history work with them and to
remove their account and all owned data. The live About page currently promises
complete export even though that lifecycle is not implemented or proven.

## Current truth

Person context-pack exports, local raw artifacts/Markdown exports, low-level
record operations, and API-key revocation exist. There is no complete owner
vault export, retention policy, `/settings/data`, or `/delete-account` route.
Production probes return 404 for those routes. The complete mapping across
Clerk identity, Convex rows, raw artifacts, generated exports, media/storage,
public links, AI grants, and audit retention is unknown.

## Scope

- Inventory every owner-scoped store and define export, retention, deletion,
  legal/audit exception, and failure-recovery behavior.
- Give the owner a complete inventory they can view and correct before export
  or deletion; surface active AI, sharing, and handoff grants in the same data
  boundary.
- Add a clear export preview and machine-readable download at `/settings/data`.
- Add confirmation, reauthentication, progress, partial-failure recovery, and
  final receipt for `/delete-account`.
- Revoke keys/links and remove public material without weakening necessary
  security audit evidence.
- Keep identity deletion site-specific; do not imply deletion from other Assist
  products or genealogy providers.

## Next safe action

Write and independently review the cross-store lifecycle map before adding any
destructive mutation. Use fixtures and preview environments first.

## Unknowns and access limits

- Exact production storage providers, retention obligations, orphan cleanup,
  backups, and Clerk deletion authority require provider verification.
- Account deletion is destructive and requires explicit action-time approval
  for production testing.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence must show a complete store inventory, deterministic export manifest,
fixture deletion across every owned store, idempotent retry, revoked access,
public disappearance, retained-minimum audit policy, and provider receipts.

## Definition of done

- [ ] `/settings/data` exports all documented owner data in open formats.
- [ ] The owner can view and correct retained data and inspect/revoke active AI,
  sharing, and handoff grants.
- [ ] The export manifest distinguishes canonical records, evidence, raw artifacts, media, and audit history.
- [ ] `/delete-account` explains scope and requires strong confirmation/reauthentication.
- [ ] Deletion is idempotent, recoverable from partial failure, and revokes public/access surfaces.
- [ ] Provider, schema, privacy, destructive-action, PR/CI/deployment, and independent audit proof exist.
