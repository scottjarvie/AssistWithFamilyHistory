## Why this exists

The core Family History job is not merely storing records; it is deciding which
claims those records support while preserving disagreement and uncertainty.

## Current truth

Imports create citation-backed `sourceFacts` with candidate, accepted, conflict,
and rejected statuses. Context packs and person workspaces display them, and
conflicts do not silently overwrite canonical person facts. No complete user
workflow is verified for comparing evidence, accepting/rejecting a candidate,
recording a reason, choosing a current conclusion, or revisiting that decision
without erasing history.

## Scope

- Let a researcher compare candidate values with their exact sources/citations.
- Accept, reject, dispute, or supersede a claim with reason, confidence, actor,
  time, and preserved prior state.
- Update canonical conclusions only through explicit reviewed promotion.
- Show conflicting evidence in person, story-readiness, Queue, context-pack,
  and publication review surfaces.
- Keep AI suggestions separate from evidence and human conclusions.

## Next safe action

Inventory current status writers/readers and design the minimum append-only
review event before adding mutation or canonical-promotion behavior.

## Unknowns and access limits

Generalized claim identity, multi-source grouping, canonical rollback, and
historical version presentation remain In design. Use synthetic conflicts.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes agreeing/conflicting/duplicate sources, review
events, promotion and rollback, source drill-through, actor attribution,
context-pack/story propagation, and cross-owner denial.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Candidate claims can be compared and adjudicated with explicit reasons.
- [ ] Canonical facts change only through reviewed promotion.
- [ ] Rejected, disputed, and superseded history remains inspectable.
- [ ] Source and AI-derived assertions stay visually and structurally distinct.
- [ ] Downstream Queue, context, story, and publish views reflect current truth.
- [ ] Schema, migration, PR/CI/deployment, live proof, and audit exist.
