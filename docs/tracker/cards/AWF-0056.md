## Why this exists

Every genealogist expects to see and move around the family picture. Family
History has the relationship data but no tree.

## Current truth

Relationships are stored and listed inside the Person page. There is no pedigree,
descendant or family-group view (AWF-0024 covers timeline, relationship and
place views more broadly).

## Scope

- Pedigree, descendants and family-group views centred on any person; proposed
  and disputed relationships drawn differently from accepted ones.
- Phone: family-group card with movement up to parents and down to children.
- Click to open a Person or re-centre.

## Next safe action

Prototype the family-group card and pedigree on synthetic data; agree the
home-person rule from AWF-0052 decision 4.

- 2026-10-06: Recorded from the read-only 6 October audits summarized in [the Site Guide](../../planning/site-guide.md#where-family-history-stands-6-october-2026). No application, provider or account change.

- [ ] Tree opens from People and every Person page, desktop and phone.
