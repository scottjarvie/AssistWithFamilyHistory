## Why this exists

Context research — a famine, a migration, a religious movement, an occupation —
touches many ancestors. Saved once on a Topic or Place, it can enrich every life
it touches, always labelled as context rather than evidence.

## Current truth

`contextItems` and `historicalContext` plus place/era packs exist and show on
person pages. There is no Topic record or page.

## Scope

- Topic record (kind, period, places, sources, review state) and Topics
  collection and page.
- Context shown on Place pages by era and interleaved on person timelines, always
  labelled as historical context.
- Migrate existing context items and packs.

## Next safe action

After AWF-0052, map context items and packs onto Topic and Place context.

- 2026-10-06: Recorded from the read-only 6 October audits summarized in [the Site Guide](../../planning/site-guide.md#where-family-history-stands-6-october-2026). No application, provider or account change.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A Topic page shows its sourced overview, places, people and stories.
