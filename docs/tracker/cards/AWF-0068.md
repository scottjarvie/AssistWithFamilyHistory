## Why this exists

The only media surface is the Person page's Memories tab, which renders PDFs and
audio as broken images. Families need to see photos, read scans, hear interviews,
pick a portrait, and add a headstone photo straight from the phone.

## Current truth

`MediaPrivacyReviewPanel` renders every item as an image; no lightbox, PDF viewer
or audio player; no portrait field; no scans on source pages; single file input
without camera capture; one-at-a-time uploads with no recovery.

## Scope

- Thumbnails from the tiny size, lightbox, PDF viewer, audio player.
- Portrait chosen from a person's photos; story cover and illustrations through
  the existing publication gate.
- Scans on Source pages; place and headstone photos on Place pages.
- Phone capture (camera, Another, paste, drop) and upload recovery for 20+ files
  (durable staging, per-file retry, resume).
- Rename the Memories tab per AWF-0052 decision 6.

## Next safe action

Build the viewer on the B2 display sizes once AWF-0065 lands.

## Evidence

- 2026-10-06: Recorded from the read-only 6 October audits summarized in [the Site Guide](../../planning/site-guide.md#where-family-history-stands-6-october-2026). No application, provider or account change.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Photos, PDFs and audio each display correctly on desktop and phone.
- [ ] A person sets a portrait and sees scans on Source pages.
- [ ] A 30-photo upload from a phone survives a dropped connection.
