## Why this exists

Operation Cleanup, Stage 3. PR #86 is being closed; this card keeps its idea.

## What it does

The person's AI, signed in to FamilySearch in their own browser, walks their
tree and sends it in batches of up to 500 records
(`family_history_import_tree_batch`), keyed by FamilySearch ids. Each batch has
per-item results, `dryRun`, `batchIndex` and the AI's cursor, so an interrupted
walk resumes (`family_history_get_tree_import`). Re-running refreshes the same
records; possible duplicates are suggested, never silently merged. New tables
`notes`, `treeImports`, `treeImportBatches`. A 5,000-person synthetic test
passed with zero failed rows.
