## Why this exists

Linear may remain dated provenance, but current runtime copy and validation
must not teach future AIs or users that external issue ids still route or prove work.

## Current truth

AGENTS and the tracker correctly make Cards, Work Orders, and Guide authoritative.
Historical QA reports retain useful GEN references. However, the signed-in
Timeline placeholder renders `GEN-24` as a product badge, and current
`check-story-fixtures` and `check-public-beta-launch` scripts require lists of
GEN ids in fixture manifests. Those validators make historical Linear labels a
current mechanical dependency even though they do not query Linear.

## Scope

- Remove external issue ids from current user-facing labels and validator gates.
- Replace necessary traceability with stable capability names, tracker Cards,
  acceptance categories, or dated historical evidence paths.
- Preserve old Linear references only inside clearly historical reports and
  immutable provenance where removal would erase useful context.
- Search current instructions, scripts, fixtures, docs, and runtime copy for
  other routing/authorization assumptions.

## Next safe action

Classify every Linear/GEN reference as historical provenance, current runtime,
validator dependency, or stale instruction before editing anything.

## Unknowns and access limits

No Linear query or reconnection is needed or permitted. Some historic fixtures
may require a replacement evidence key rather than simple deletion.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes a complete reference classification, validator
fixtures that pass without Linear ids, runtime text checks, no-current-routing
search, and preserved dated provenance.

## Definition of done

- [ ] No current UI, instruction, validator, or fixture requires Linear identity.
- [ ] Tracker/Card or capability keys replace any needed current traceability.
- [ ] Historical reports retain useful provenance and are labeled historical.
- [ ] Full verification passes without querying or depending on Linear.
- [ ] Normal PR, CI, deployment/live copy proof, and independent audit exist.
