## Why this exists

Scott (21 October 2026, unattended run brief): a person's AI should be able to
sign in to a genealogy site such as FamilySearch on the person's behalf and
map their entire tree into Family History, thousands of people with every
fact, relationship, source, citation, note, media reference and outside id,
not just names and dates. The existing batch tool was sized for one census
page.

## Current truth

- Three MCP tools: `family_history_begin_tree_import`,
  `family_history_import_tree_batch` (up to 500 records per call, dry run,
  per-item results) and `family_history_get_tree_import` (progress, cursor,
  recent batches, possible duplicates).
- Every record is keyed by its outside id; resending updates, never
  duplicates. Records the person entered are never overwritten; the import
  fills blanks and reports what it kept.
- Possible duplicates become proposed `possibly_same_as` connections. Nothing
  is merged.
- Schema: `notes` on any record, `treeImports`, `treeImportBatches`,
  `externalIds` and `importJobId` on the main record tables, citation links to
  notes and media.

## Next safe action

AWF-0072: a screen for the possible-duplicate suggestions and imported notes.
Then a live proof: Scott's own AI imports a small FamilySearch branch.

## Constraints

Family History never contacts FamilySearch. Whole-workspace connections only.
Media arrives as references, no bytes.

## Completion evidence

2026-10-21: `convex/treeImport.test.ts` imports a synthetic 5,000-person tree
in 500-record batches with zero failed rows, plus idempotency, replay, dry
run, resume, linking and duplicate cases. Draft PR on
`claude/familyhistory-20261021`; not deployed.
