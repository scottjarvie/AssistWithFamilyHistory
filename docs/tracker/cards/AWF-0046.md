## Why this exists

AWF-0045 closed the urgent gap: a reviewed scan can reach a chosen AI as real
bytes. The family standard adds the durable storage and lifecycle guarantees
that Convex `storageId` alone does not express. Family-history evidence also
needs a deliberate distinction between a source-exact archival artifact and
the cleaned rendition safe for normal viewing and AI use.

## Current truth

- Person uploads land in private Convex Storage and new/replaced files become
  Private, unreviewed, and unavailable to AI.
- `family_history_get_evidence` applies owner, boundary, living/review,
  `aiUseAllowed`, rights, and byte-budget gates.
- The baseline row had no B2 version/hash/derivative manifest or durable upload
  session. The active branch replaces the unused public-URL helper with a
  private, exact-version evidence path; deployed rows remain unchanged.
- Existing files and imported references are real product state. This Proposed
  Card authorizes no provider action or migration.
- The active branch now has the additive exact-manifest/session control plane,
  fail-closed private/public bucket bindings, source-exact private image
  originals, three metadata-clean renditions, exact-version/hash/type/length/
  decode checks, partial-write cleanup, and structured camera/GPS proposals.
- The production code and Convex schema/actions are deployed after the native
  `sharp` package hotfix. Backblaze inventory confirmed that no Family History
  buckets exist, but its write session expired before resource creation;
  Convex has no B2 bindings, so uploads fail closed and no live byte or
  migration was attempted.

## Next safe action

After the person signs in to Backblaze, inventory existing buckets and keys
before creating anything. Then configure exactly one private and one public
project bucket, prove least-privilege denial and a marked synthetic image byte,
and extend the validated rendition policy to the remaining media classes before
any migration proposal.

## Constraints

- Private only. Public story derivatives remain a separate human publication
  program.
- Storage keys contain no person, family, place, title, living status, or
  relationship text.
- Ordinary UI/MCP reads never receive the restricted archival original.
- Upload never sets review, AI-use permission, rights approval, fact truth,
  conflict settlement, or publication.
- No silent fallback to Convex Storage or the public-URL B2 helper.

## Completion evidence

- Tests cover exact manifests, checksum/type/full-decode mismatch,
  metadata/provenance extraction, clean renditions, replay/conflict, expiry,
  concurrent finish, superseded versions, delete, and no-fallback.
- Owner/people/work boundary and living-person refusals stay uniform.
- A provider inventory and least-privilege denial can be run without exposing
  secrets, but is recorded as a later proof rung.
- A migration dry-run compares exact counts/hashes and changes no current byte.

On 2026-09-02, local tests passed for exact manifests, byte/type/hash/decode
validation, clean renditions, bounded partial-write cleanup, session replay,
single-finalizer concurrency, and revoke-before-finish. Typecheck, lint,
production build, tracker parity, and all media/trust/API boundary contracts
also passed. Provider, migration, deployed-byte, and operational cleanup proof
remain deliberately unclaimed.
