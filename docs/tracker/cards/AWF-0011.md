## Why this exists

Search and AI crawlers should discover the public Family History explanation,
not protected vault URLs that immediately become duplicate sign-in pages. This
does not expose vault data today, but it weakens public truth, crawl quality,
and the privacy posture of the signed-in shell.

## Current truth

The live sitemap contains `/app` plus protected people, places, imports,
operations, research, story-writer, and settings routes. `robots.txt` allows
all paths. A fresh 18-page public crawl followed eight protected URLs through
307 redirects to the same Clerk sign-in surface, producing duplicate titles,
descriptions, dead ends, and false missing-landmark results. Browser checks
confirmed sign-in rather than vault disclosure; no private record was read.

## Scope

- Restrict the sitemap to genuinely public, indexable routes.
- Add intentional robots and page-metadata boundaries for protected app, auth,
  API, preview, and non-indexable story surfaces.
- Keep current public `/llms.txt` and `/context-schema` discoverable only to the
  extent their claims remain accurate and data-free.
- Preserve readable public story policy and its beta `noindex` default.

## Next safe action

Write a route-classification table from the protected-route source, sitemap,
robots, metadata, and public-story policy; then change all surfaces together in
one normal PR.

## Unknowns and access limits

- The crawl tool warned that site protection may make some results incomplete.
- Its Stripe publishable-key warning is not treated as a secret finding; public
  client keys require context and no private key was observed.
- Authenticated content was not crawled or inspected.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence must include route-classification fixtures, regenerated sitemap and
robots output, crawler results, direct HTTP/meta checks, public-story indexing
tests, and confirmation that protected routes still require auth.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Sitemap entries are public, canonical, and intentionally indexable.
- [ ] Protected app/auth/API routes have explicit crawl and indexing boundaries.
- [ ] Public story noindex/index rules remain separate and tested.
- [ ] A fresh crawl no longer treats protected routes as duplicate public pages.
- [ ] Public truth guides remain reachable and contain no vault data.
- [ ] PR, full CI, deployment, live metadata, and independent audit proof exist.
