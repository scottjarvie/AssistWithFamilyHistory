## Why this exists

Genealogy research is organized around questions (*who were her parents?*) and
lives or dies on its research log, including searches that found nothing. Today
that work is split between research tasks, research checks, the research log and
the operations view, with no place for the person's own Notes or for saved AI
Research reports.

## Current truth

`researchTasks`, `researchChecks` and `researchLog` exist with their own
vocabulary; `/app/research` and `/app/operations` show them. There is no Note
(person-only) or Research (AI-only) record and no Question page.

## Scope

- Question record: the question in the person's words, subjects (people, places,
  sources), status (open, being worked, answered, parked), nested Questions,
  Plan items, research log entries (searched / found / not found), linked
  Research, the conclusion and its reasoning.
- Note (person-authored only) and Research (AI-authored only, with sources, the
  AI's name and Preferences used); both attach to any record.
- Questions collection and Question page; Your work page with Notes, Research,
  Stories tabs.
- Migrate existing research tasks/checks/log rows into Questions without losing
  history; retire the overlapping views.

## Next safe action

After AWF-0052 is answered, map every current research-task/check/log field to
the Question model and write the migration dry-run against a synthetic vault.
