## Why this exists

Operation Cleanup, Stage 3. Where each real page stands against the Family
Folio design. Source: `find app -name page.tsx` on `main` `d2b0552` (52 files),
matched to `design-council/2026-11-05-family-folio/page-inventory.md` and its
"Council status" column ("Pending" = untouched). The page families come from
the design council's **Assist design playbook**
(`assist-design-playbook.md` in the design-council skill's references; it lives
outside this repository).

Status key: **redesigned** = built on the folio kit and accepted by an Opus
review (remaining polish and owed reviews live in AWF-0078); **partly** =
built, acceptance or a product decision still pending; **untouched** = not yet
worked on; **retire, no redesign** = the Site Guide retires it or it is legacy.
A "Decide first" note is the product question to settle before any design work.

Counts: 47 routes — 7 redesigned, 7 partly, 20 untouched,
13 retire, no redesign.
