## Why this exists

When imports, AI calls, auth, or publishing fail, maintainers need enough safe
evidence to diagnose the problem without exposing private family material or
asking users to repeat risky actions.

## Current truth

User-facing Convex and OpenRouter errors are sanitized, local contracts pass,
and Vercel deployment/live health can be inspected. The privacy review says
structured server logging still needs a production destination. This audit did
not inspect current production logs, alerting, error grouping, request-id
propagation, retention, or incident response. No current outage was observed.

## Scope

- Define request/correlation ids across browser, Next routes, Convex, AI calls,
  imports, and publishing without logging sensitive payloads.
- Group actionable errors and record environment, release, route, safe actor
  class, outcome, and provider correlation.
- Add privacy review, retention/access controls, alert thresholds, and a concise
  incident handoff/rollback runbook.
- Keep product activity history separate from diagnostic telemetry.

## Next safe action

Write a data-minimizing event schema and provider comparison before enabling
any external log destination or alert.

## Unknowns and access limits

Current provider plan, logging destination, billing impact, production volume,
retention, and on-call workflow require owner/provider decisions. No secrets or
private logs may be copied into the tracker.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes synthetic error fixtures, request-id continuity,
redaction tests, provider receipt, retention/access settings, grouped incidents,
alert and recovery drill, release correlation, and no user-content leakage.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Key routes produce correlated, privacy-safe diagnostic events.
- [ ] Errors group by actionable cause and exact release/environment.
- [ ] Logs exclude tokens, raw captures, family content, and living-person data.
- [ ] Retention, access, billing, alerts, and incident ownership are explicit.
- [ ] One synthetic incident is detected, handed off, resolved, and audited.
- [ ] Provider/config changes use normal safeguards and fresh authorization.
