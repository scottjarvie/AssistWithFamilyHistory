## Why this exists

The source is the heart of family history: the record that makes a fact
believable. Today sources, citations, source facts and media are separate tables
and no page shows a source with its scan, transcription and the facts it
supports.

## Current truth

`sources`, `citations`, `citationLinks`, `sourceFacts` and `media` exist.
Media can point at a source (`media.sourceId`) but nothing shows it. The only
media surface is the Person page's Memories tab.

## Scope

- Sources collection (Table/Grid/List; Grid is the photo gallery) with the
  Site Guide's filters.
- Source page: original viewer, citation, transcription/translation, the Claims it
  states (each linked to a subject), people and places mentioned, review,
  rights, AI-readability, origin and history.
- Every uploaded file belongs to a Source or is a portrait chosen from one; an
  upload with no context creates a photograph Source.
- Family-memory Sources (who told it, when) can support Claims labelled as lore.
- Claim states Proposed · Accepted · Disputed · Rejected shown with conflicts side
  by side on the Person page (with AWF-0027).

## Next safe action

After AWF-0052, design the Source ↔ File ↔ Citation ↔ Claim shape and the
migration from current media rows.
