## Current truth

`lib/stories/publishSafety.ts` `totalCitationCount` takes the larger of the
story's recorded citation-ID count and the citation count in the person's
returned evidence groups. The evidence gate then says those citations
“support the draft.” This can mix current person-source coverage with
provenance recorded when the draft was saved. The existing bundle is also
capped. This behavior predates the design tranche; no production story or
private source was inspected and no publication-policy change is claimed.

The new private Story reader separately identifies whole-draft recorded IDs,
attached research packs and other person evidence. That presentation does not
change `publishSafety` or establish paragraph-level support.

## Next safe action

Add an offline fixture with zero recorded draft citations and abundant current
person evidence. Inspect the returned evidence gate, counts and copy. Establish
whether current coverage is intentionally sufficient for the existing publish
gate or whether draft provenance is required; propose a bounded correction
with explicit compatibility and human-review expectations. Keep this out of
the visual-only council fix and do not weaken any privacy/publish guard.
