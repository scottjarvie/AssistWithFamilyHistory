## Why this exists

The person's own uploads still go to legacy Convex storage with no display sizes,
are served raw with location data, and pass through a Vercel function capped
near 4.5 MB while the site promises 25 MB. A phone photo or a scan is the most
ordinary thing a family historian uploads.

## Current truth

`app/api/media/upload/route.ts` buffers `formData` into Convex storage;
`app/api/mcp-media-upload/.../route.ts` buffers the whole body. The B2 path makes
three metadata-free sizes for JPEG/PNG/WebP only. HEIC/TIFF are stored raw. No
per-account storage counting.

## Scope

- Confirm the live size limit first.
- Direct-to-B2 (presigned PUT or multipart) for person and AI uploads, with the
  storage CORS list; same manifest, renditions and review flow for both.
- HEIC and TIFF accepted and converted to display sizes; originals kept exact.
- Per-account byte counters with a reconcile job (feeds /me and /admin).
- Migration dry-run, then migration, for existing Convex media rows.
- Re-baseline AWF-WO-012 against media standard 1.8.0; record the sharp decision.

## Next safe action

Upload a synthetic 8 MB JPEG to production as a marked test account and record
the result.
