## Why this exists

Family-history users must know where private research is stored, what the beta
really does today, and which actions remain unfinished before they entrust the
product with source material. The public site currently gives two incompatible
storage promises and does not explain its already-public Assist relationship.

## Current truth

The stale contradictions that created this Card were removed by the 2.0.0
Assist With Family History release and its follow-up first-journey work.
`/about` and `/privacy` now describe the durable private workspace without the
old all-local promise; the public home carries a practical authority/capability
FAQ; `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, `/llms.txt`, and `/mcp` are current; and the canonical
domain, sign-in, signed-in shell, Queue, and public truth surfaces share the
Assist With Family History identity. AWF-0035, AWF-0036, and AWF-0033 preserve
the protected-release and live evidence.

## Delivered outcome

- One enduring Assist With Family History identity and canonical address.
- Consistent structured-storage, local-artifact, authority, and privacy copy.
- Working, evidence-bounded AI setup and machine-readable truth surfaces.
- Current / Partial / Later wording backed by protected and live proof.

## Next safe action

No implementation remains on this intake Card. Future wording parity belongs
to the specific surface or compatibility Card that reproduces it; do not reopen
the pre-rebrand bundle.
