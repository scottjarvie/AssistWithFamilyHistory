## Why this exists

Family-history users must know where private research is stored, what the beta
really does today, and which actions remain unfinished before they entrust the
product with source material. The public site currently gives two incompatible
storage promises and does not explain its already-public Assist relationship.

## Current truth

At production deployment `dpl_4x1uoNBMJCR1VgA33UWHFRo3F2kA`, `/about` says
all work stays on the computer, no family data is stored on servers, and
everything can be exported. `/privacy` and the current architecture correctly
say structured genealogy data uses Convex while raw capture artifacts and
exports remain local. The home page honestly calls the product a private beta,
but it has no FAQ, honest working-product image, or current `/ai` setup path.
`/ai`, `/ai.txt`, and `/mcp` return 404.

Assist With Life currently labels Assist With Family History `LIVE`, links to
`discovertheirstories.com`, and offers “Open Assist With Family History.” The
canonical Project Philosophy still records launch stage as Unknown because
that external fact had not been reverified. This Card records the fresh
evidence without treating an open domain as proof of authenticated operation.

## Scope

- Reconcile storage, export, AI-processing, and current-capability language
  across the home, About, Privacy, Features, Roadmap, and Updates surfaces.
- Preserve **Discover Their Stories** as the application identity while
  explaining its **Assist With Family History** family relationship.
- Add the Core home-page FAQ and one clearly synthetic or privacy-safe image of
  a verified working surface.
- Use Current / verified, Coming soon, In design, Later, and Unknown accurately.
- Keep unavailable AI connection controls visibly non-working or omit them;
  do not make `/llms.txt` stand in for `/ai.txt`.

## Next safe action

Prepare a normal protected software/content branch that first writes a
sentence-by-sentence capability ledger, then changes only claims that the
source and exact live environment can support.

## Unknowns and access limits

- No authenticated vault journey was available to this signed-out audit.
- Exact deployed Clerk, Convex trust-boundary mode, export completeness, and
  account-deletion behavior are not proven.
- Do not inspect real family records or use living-person material as proof.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence must include a claim matrix tied to exact routes, desktop and mobile
browser receipts, console state, accessibility checks, the deployed commit and
deployment id, and HTTP proof for every action described as working.

## Definition of done

- [ ] About and Privacy give one accurate, consistent storage explanation.
- [ ] Public capability and launch language matches verified current behavior.
- [ ] The home page has six to ten useful FAQs and one privacy-safe product image.
- [ ] The Discover Their Stories / Assist With Family History relationship is clear without erasing product identity.
- [ ] Unavailable `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, `/mcp`, export, and deletion capabilities are not implied as working.
- [ ] Public routes pass desktop/mobile, keyboard, focus, contrast, reduced-motion, overflow, and console checks.
- [ ] A normal PR, full CI, deployment, and live-route proof exist.
