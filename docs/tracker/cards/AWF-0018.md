## Why this exists

Beta users need a dependable help and problem-reporting path with the right
product context. Mailto links do not provide the shared portfolio lifecycle.

## Current truth

`/contact` offers support, feature, and privacy email links. There is no verified
Family History source key, shared Support Desk link in the shell, structured
receipt, status, or product-aware handoff. The Philosophy correctly says not to
market the desk until it is live.

## Scope

- Register Family History with the shared desk when that provider is live.
- Link product-native help entry points with safe product/route/release context.
- Explain what is sent and exclude private vault data, captures, secrets, and
  living-person details by default.
- Preserve direct privacy contact and truthful unavailable/degraded states.

## Next safe action

Verify the shared desk's public/intake state, then write the cross-repository
source-key and privacy contract before enabling links.

## Unknowns and access limits

Support intake, provider configuration, owner routing, retention, and response
workflow are unavailable here and must not be inferred.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes source registration, no-private-data payload tests,
disclosure, receipt/status behavior, disabled state, cross-repository link proof,
and support-owner handoff.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Family History has a verified shared-desk source and product-aware route.
- [ ] Users know exactly what context will be sent.
- [ ] Private family content and secrets are excluded by default.
- [ ] Receipt, status, unavailable, and privacy-escalation paths work.
- [ ] Shell links appear only after live desk proof.
- [ ] Cross-repo PR/deployment evidence and independent audit exist.
