## Why this exists

The Assist promise depends on a person being able to bring a chosen AI into a
scoped, revocable relationship with durable Family History work. Internal APIs
and key-management screens do not prove that connection.

## Current truth

AWF-0033 and AWF-WO-006 shipped the useful connection this Card requested:
stateless OAuth `/mcp`, server-derived vault identity, twelve workflow-native
tools, bounded reads, replay-safe writes, complete-result saves, corrections,
Queue continuation, `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, and `/llms.txt`. A disposable public PKCE
client completed the production workflow and cleanup. Broad client coverage,
reconnect, fresh-device email, and immediate issued-JWT revocation remain
Partial in AWF-0034 and AWF-0038 rather than keeping this old missing-foundation
Card open.

## Delivered outcome

- One real stateless MCP endpoint with bounded reads, review-first writes,
  corrections, complete-result saves, and Queue continuation.
- Server-derived identity and owner isolation; no client-selected vault.
- Current `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, and `/llms.txt` setup and authority guidance.
- One production PKCE client retrieve → work → save → inspect journey with
  exact synthetic cleanup.

## Next safe action

No foundation work remains here. Use AWF-0037 for the joined Queue result proof
and AWF-0038 for new MCP-standard compatibility and clearer endpoint guidance.

## Unknowns and access limits

- One disposable external PKCE client is proven; broad client compatibility,
  reconnect, and immediate issued-token revocation are not.
- Do not claim universal AI compatibility from one client proof.
- Do not infer external-action authority from ordinary read/write scope or a
  Queue instruction.

## Evidence

Evidence is preserved in AWF-0033, AWF-WO-006, the production acceptance record,
