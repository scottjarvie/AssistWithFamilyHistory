## Why this exists

The Assist promise depends on a person being able to bring a chosen AI into a
scoped, revocable relationship with durable Family History work. Internal APIs
and key-management screens do not prove that connection.

## Current truth

The repository has internal app APIs, API-key mint/list/suspend/revoke
primitives, scope vocabulary, a static capability manifest, a Story OpenAPI
skeleton, `/api/capabilities`, `/app/api`, `/llms.txt`, and context packs.
Incoming external-AI credential resolution and a stable public agent API are
not proven. Production `/mcp`, `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, and `/settings/ai` return 404.
`/llms.txt` says MCP and full OpenAPI are in development and cannot substitute
for a generated current AI briefing. The Queue foundation now defines seven
narrow internal tool contracts and Queue-specific scopes, but intentionally
does not expose them until an incoming credential can derive owner and actor.

## Scope

- Ship one real MCP endpoint with at least one useful read and one review-first
  write for the Family History research-to-story loop.
- Provide install, reconnect, scope, status, test, revoke, and error recovery at
  `/ai` and `/settings/ai`.
- Generate `/ai.txt` from current capability truth; keep `/llms.txt` honest.
- Enforce owner, subject/context, operation, privacy, and review boundaries at
  the backend; record meaningful AI activity.
- Prove one chosen-AI retrieve → work → save → inspect result journey.

## Next safe action

Choose the smallest useful tool pair and write its threat model and scope
matrix before implementing endpoint or key-resolution code.

## Unknowns and access limits

- No external AI client, incoming bearer-key resolution, quota, request-id,
  usage, or complete audit path is verified.
- Do not claim universal AI compatibility from one client proof.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence must include protocol tests, scoped authorization fixtures, revocation,
cross-owner denial, privacy filtering, generated-guide parity, one real client
connection, saved-result inspection, PR/CI/deployment, and independent audit.

## Definition of done

- [ ] `/mcp` exposes a minimal useful, scoped Family History tool set.
- [ ] `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, and `/settings/ai` describe only current capabilities.
- [ ] Connect, test, revoke, expired, forbidden, and provider-error states work.
- [ ] Backend identity and operation scopes fail closed across owner boundaries.
- [ ] Meaningful AI work is visible in record/activity history.
- [ ] A real chosen-AI round trip is proven without implying every AI works.
