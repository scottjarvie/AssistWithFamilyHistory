## Why this exists

The product owner eventually needs aggregate service health, usage, support,
and public-content operations without reading private family records.

## Current truth

Production `/admin` returns 404. `/app/api/admin` is a narrow current-vault API
key operator view and explicitly is not cross-tenant administration. Provider
health and support/public-content operations are not combined safely.

## Scope

- Provide owner-only aggregate users, usage, health, public-content, abuse/
  support, and provider signals with strict role enforcement.
- Use privacy-safe aggregates; do not read private family content for totals.
- Record operator actions and high-risk confirmations.
- Keep current-vault key management and ordinary `/me` separate.

## Next safe action

Define the minimum owner decisions and no-private-data metric contract before
adding queries or provider integrations.

## Unknowns and access limits

Operator identity, provider APIs, support intake, incident process, and safe
aggregate availability need future design. No provider change is authorized.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes role denials, metric lineage, no-private-content
review, operator events, degraded-provider states, and synthetic browser proof.

## Definition of done

- [ ] `/admin` is owner-only and separate from `/me` and current-vault tools.
- [ ] Aggregate metrics have documented privacy-safe lineage.
- [ ] No private family content is read or rendered for ordinary statistics.
- [ ] Operator changes require confirmation and are audited.
- [ ] Provider outages and unavailable metrics remain truthful.
- [ ] Security, PR/CI/deployment, live proof, and independent audit exist.
