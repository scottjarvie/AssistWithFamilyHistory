## Why this exists

Lists are how people gather work their own way (*graves to photograph, records to
order, photos to identify*). Preferences are how the person tells any AI how they
want help. Both are family pages Family History lacks.

## Current truth

No List or Preferences record exists. `/app/settings` is a legacy page storing an
OpenRouter key in the browser, not Preferences.

## Scope

- Lists collection and List page; entries point at real records with an optional
  note, order and check-off; the AI can create and maintain Lists.
- Preferences page: research depth, original records over indexes, priority
  lines, story tone, living-relative handling; readable by the AI; clearly
  separate from connection permissions.

## Next safe action

Reuse a sibling site's List and Preferences shapes where they fit Family History
records.

## Evidence

- 2026-10-06: Recorded from the read-only 6 October audits summarized in [the Site Guide](../../planning/site-guide.md#where-family-history-stands-6-october-2026). No application, provider or account change.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A person and their AI can create, order and check off Lists of real records.
- [ ] Preferences can be edited by the person and read by the AI.
- [ ] Live on the production site.
