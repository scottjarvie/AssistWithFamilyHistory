## Acceptance

- Both arrangements retain all current destinations, active route and count meanings.
- Switching preserves search, filters, collection view and unfinished Queue/form text.
- Navigation and theme work independently; the navigation preference is browser-local under the current Core, not an account or family sync claim.
- Phone navigation remains usable with all destinations reachable, and returning to a wide window restores the desktop choice.
- Record list/detail, Queue draft, reload, keyboard, theme and responsive checks; keep prototype and deployed proof separate.

## Future suggestion

Optional family-wide appearance defaults with per-site overrides may be useful,
but are not approved identity sharing or a synchronization service. The present
request does not alter private family records, account access or data boundaries.
