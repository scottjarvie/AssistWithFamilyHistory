## Why this exists

`/contact`, `/privacy`, and the marketing call to action published
`support@`, `features@`, and `privacy@discovertheirstories.com` addresses. No
Assist With site runs a mailbox or an email server, so every one of those links
was a promise the product could not keep — the worst kind of honesty failure on
a page a worried person reaches when they need help with family records.

Scott's portfolio decision is that all support for the Assist With family runs
through the one central desk at `https://assistwithlife.com/support`, and that
each site must make that evident rather than implying private email contact.

## Current truth

Every public surface now sends people and AI agents to the one support desk and
says plainly that direct email contact is not available:

- `/contact` — the intro names Assist With Life Support and the honesty
  sentence; the three topic cards (support, feature requests, privacy) each open
  the desk.
- `/privacy` — the Contact section routes policy questions to the desk instead
  of a privacy mailbox, so the policy stays honest about how to reach a human.
- The marketing CTA "Share feedback" button opens the desk, with the support
  sentence beneath it.
- `/llms.txt` and `/ai.txt` tell an AI agent where support lives and instruct it
  never to tell a person to email this product.

`lib/site/supportDesk.ts` is the single source of the desk URL, label, and
honesty sentence. `pnpm check:support-contact` fails the build if a `mailto:`
link or a contact address returns to `app/`, `components/`, `features/`, or
`lib/`, or if any of those four surfaces stops pointing at the desk.

The home FAQ carried no email-contact claim, so its eight questions are
unchanged. `https://assistwithlife.com/support` returned HTTP 200 with the
title "Support desk · Assist With Life" before the links were written.

## Boundaries

- No mailbox, forwarding address, contact form, or ticket integration is
  implied or built here; this Card only routes people to the existing desk.
- The registered Assist With Life Support Desk source key and the header/footer
  desk link in the shell remain unbuilt. The Project Philosophy Support Desk row
  records that remaining gap.
