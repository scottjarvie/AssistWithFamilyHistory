## Why this exists

Reading old handwriting needs detail. The AI should never be refused a photo
because it is large, and should be able to look at a region or skim a large set.

## Current truth

Convex rows over 2 MiB return `TOO_LARGE` (`convex/mcpEvidence.ts`); the AI gets
only the 1200 px copy; no region crop or contact sheet; errors collapse to
`INTERNAL_ERROR`; `/ai.txt` lacks the hostname allowlist and new-conversation note.

## Scope

- Serve a size that fits; large copy and region crops on request; contact sheets
  and cursor paging for large sets.
- Standard error names (`invalid_image`, `upload_incomplete`, `media_unavailable`,
  `session_expired`).
- PDF page renders and audio delivery on the B2 path; optional captioned pending
  slot for clients that cannot make HTTP requests.
- `/ai.txt` media guidance.

## Next safe action

Add region and large-size options to the evidence read tool behind tests.
