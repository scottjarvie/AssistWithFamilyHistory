## Decision

Scott answered all nine on 6 October 2026, one at a time in the project thread. They are recorded in the Site Guide's [Decisions](../../planning/site-guide.md#decisions) table, with the options and recommendations in [`site-guide-content.json`](../../planning/site-guide-content.json).

1. Site Guide canonical: **yes** (as recommended).
2. Research unit: **Projects and Questions**. Projects hold Questions; Questions still nest. (Recommended: Questions only.)
3. Name: **Claims**. (Recommended: Facts.)
4. Trees: **several trees**, each a separate workspace switched like a profile; Resources, Preferences, Instructions, the AI connection and the Queue carry across trees. (Recommended: one workspace.)
5. Photos: **inside Sources**, connectable to every person, place, event, Project and Story they matter to (as recommended, extended).
6. Memories: **Notes and family-memory Sources**; rename the Memories tab (as recommended).
7. Topics: **yes** (as recommended).
8. Connecting an AI: **sign-in approves**, with the hard limits and living-person/private-memory protections (as recommended).
9. Sharing: **not yet** (as recommended).

New open question raised by 4: Topics at account level (recommended) or per tree.

## Why Scott was needed

Scott is about to start entering real family data. The building blocks decide
what that data *is* — whether research is organized by Questions or Projects,
what a "fact" is called, whether there is one tree or several, where photos and
family memories live. These are product and language choices, and changing them
after data exists turns an edit into a migration.

## Smallest decision or action

Answer the nine numbered decisions in the Site Guide's
[Decisions Scott needs to make](../../planning/site-guide.md#decisions-scott-needs-to-make)
table. "All recommended" is a complete answer; any single item can be changed.

## Recommendation

Accept all nine recommendations: make the Site Guide canonical; Questions as the
research unit; "Facts" in the interface; one workspace per account; photos inside
Sources; memories as Notes or family-memory Sources; Topics for reusable
context; sign-in is the approval with publish/delete/merge/share impossible for
any AI; no new sharing in the first builds.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

- **Projects instead of Questions:** familiar container, but adds a second
  research unit beside Questions and invites empty folders.
- **"Claims" instead of "Facts":** more precise genealogically, colder for a
  relative with a box of photos.
- **Multiple trees:** matches some desktop software, but splits people and
  sources and is hard to undo.
- **Separate Photos collection:** easier to explain, but files then drift apart
  from the evidence they are.
- **Keep the seven-permission approval:** maximum caution, but contradicts the
  family rule and is the main first-connection friction.

## What each choice changes

Decisions 2–7 set the schema and page list for AWF-WO-015 and AWF-WO-016.
Decision 8 sets the connection behaviour in AWF-WO-017. Decision 9 keeps
AWF-0014/0015 in backlog. Decision 1 decides which document builders read first.

## Safe default

Until Scott answers, builders may do only work that is the same under every
option (MCP conformance, media storage fixes, Queue lifecycle retrofit). No
schema change for the new blocks starts.

## Consequence of waiting

Data entered before these are settled will need migrating, and AWF-WO-015/016
cannot start.
