## Why this exists

A small set of proven family-history starting patterns can help a person and
their chosen AI begin useful work without turning Family History into a rigid
automation engine.

## Current truth

Product documents mention research planners and prompt exports, but no verified
curated library, entry metadata contract, freshness review, or chosen-AI
library path exists. A prompt reference is not proof of a shipped loop.

## Scope

- Keep a prompt as one editable instruction and a research loop as a reusable
  repeat pattern; never blur either into autonomous site-owned execution.
- Make every entry optional, editable, combinable, and ignorable by the person
  or their chosen AI. An entry is not a policy, required route, or permission.
- Cover a small useful menu: research plan; evidence/same-person comparison;
  research gaps; source-aware story outline; place/era context; living-relative
  interview questions; supplied-source translation/transcription/interpretation;
  and a tree/visual/story work brief.
- Record version, created date, last reviewed/updated date, purpose,
  author/maintainer when meaningful, and known assumptions/limits.
- Keep current-capability and chosen-AI compatibility claims evidence-bounded.

## Next safe action

Define the entry schema and curate three high-value examples with synthetic
inputs before designing discovery UI or any execution behavior.

## Unknowns and access limits

Storage, review ownership, client compatibility, import/export format, and
whether entries remain repository-owned or user-saveable are In design. Do not
use private family material in examples.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes schema validation, optional/edit/combine/ignore
scenarios, stale-version labeling, provenance display, safe synthetic examples,
and proof that using a prompt or loop neither grants workspace authority nor
starts autonomous work.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Prompt and research-loop concepts remain visibly distinct.
- [ ] Every entry is optional, editable, combinable, and ignorable.
- [ ] Version, dates, purpose, provenance/maintainer, and assumptions/limits are visible.
- [ ] No entry grants permission, implies compatibility, or runs autonomously.
- [ ] Current/Later/Unknown claims, PR/CI/deployment, live proof, and independent audit are accurate.
