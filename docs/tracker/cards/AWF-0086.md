## Current truth

### Research home

- **Projects, Questions, Notes and Research** (AWF-0053). `researchTasks`,
  `researchChecks` and `researchLog` hold today's research; there is no
  Project, Question page, person-only Note or AI-only Research record. First:
  map each field to the Question model and dry-run the migration on a synthetic
  vault.
- **Topics** (AWF-0055). `contextItems`, `historicalContext` and place/era
  packs exist; no Topic record or page.
- **Lists, Preferences and several trees** (AWF-0057, AWF-WO-015). No List or
  Preferences record; trees as separate workspaces are decided but not built.
  Reuse a sibling site's shapes.

### Archive and evidence

- **Sources as the family archive** (AWF-0054). `sources`, `citations`,
  `citationLinks`, `sourceFacts` and `media.sourceId` exist; no Source page
  shows them together.
- **Claims adjudication** (AWF-0027). `sourceFacts` carry candidate, accepted,
  conflict and rejected, but there is no review workflow with a reason and
  history. The Site Guide names these Claims: Proposed, Accepted, Disputed,
  Rejected.
- **Search across the vault** (AWF-0023). Only narrow per-page search exists.
- **Activity history** (AWF-0013). No plain-language entry for every
  meaningful create, change, delete, merge, publish or AI action; the Queue's
  own history is the only complete one.

### Views

- **Tree** (AWF-0056) and **timeline, relationship and place views**
  (AWF-0024). `/app/timeline` is a placeholder; no pedigree, family group or
  map.
- **Dense collections** (AWF-0021). The design council added facets and saved
  views; cursor pagination and sortable comparison tables are still missing
  (People loads 50-row increments, Places has no paging).

### Shared pages and operations

- **Delete the whole workspace** (AWF-0074, AWF-0010). No deletion code;
  `OWNED_TABLES` (`convex/vaultMigration.ts:33`) is the list; B2 needs a
  version-exact delete in `lib/media/evidenceB2.ts` (shared with AWF-0084).
  Plan: `deleting` account state gating every byte path, batched resumable
  deletes, typed confirmation on `/app/settings/data`, Sandbox proof first.
- **Owner operations view at `/admin`** (AWF-0017). Not built; no cross-tenant
  view, provider health or no-private-data metrics.
- **Support source key** (AWF-0018). `/support` links the shared desk
  (`lib/site/supportDesk.ts:14`) without a Family History source key.
- **Assist family wayfinding** (AWF-0019). No family row in header or footer.
- **Production observability** (AWF-0028). No structured log destination,
  alerting or request ids; write a data-minimizing event schema first.
- **Sharing, after the core blocks are in use** (AWF-0014, AWF-0015). Site
  Guide decision (6 Oct 2026): no new sharing in the first builds; Unlisted
  links and Trusted collaborators later, one tree at a time.
