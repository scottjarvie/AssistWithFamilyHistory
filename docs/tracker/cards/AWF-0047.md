## Why this exists

A chosen AI may locate a public-domain scan, receive a document from the person,
or produce an attributed private artifact during research. Today it cannot put
those bytes into the durable vault without asking the person to repeat the
upload. Passing a normal file through model-text base64 is not reliable.

## Current truth

- Existing tools cover brief/search/context, Queue, protected batch evidence,
  batch records, complete result, and corrections.
- Person upload and evidence retrieval already preserve review, rights,
  AI-use, living-person, and record-boundary gates.
- On the retained production baseline, no
  `family_history_begin_evidence_upload` or
  `family_history_finish_evidence_upload` tool exists. Both are implemented
  locally on the active branch but are not deployed or named-client-proven.
- The active branch now adds both tools behind a separate
  `family_history:evidence:write` grant. Begin binds person, optional source,
  type, length, SHA-256, operation identity, and provenance hints to a durable
  session; its returned URL is a short-lived first-party relay capability.
  Finish rechecks the grant and session, verifies the stored original, creates
  clean renditions, and returns a pending-person-review receipt.
- The finished row is Private, unreviewed, rights-unknown, and AI-use-off. The
  signed-in review UI shows camera/GPS proposals and lets the person accept or
  decline them without implying publication.

## Next safe action

Run the image-only flow against marked provider-backed bytes after Backblaze
inventory and configuration, then deploy behind normal review and verify one
supported named client in a new connection. In parallel, specify and implement
PDF/transcript/audio/video plus resumable multi-file behavior without widening
research or story-write scopes.

## Constraints

- Direct PUT is normal; inline is tiny-image-only after named-client proof.
- No caller-supplied owner id, provider coordinate, raw storage URL, review,
  rights approval, living-person override, conflict settlement, identity merge,
  access change, or publication.
- Reference-only FamilySearch media remain user-mediated and honestly
  unavailable; no credential/session capture or unattended mirroring.
- Preserve the existing skip reasons and add truthful upload errors without
  leaking existence.

## Completion evidence

- Begin/finish re-resolve the current grant, people/work boundary, target
  relationships, and upload state.
- Images, one PDF/transcript, and one audio fixture complete without model-text
  bytes; over-budget video follows the documented resource fallback.
- The new item is visible to the person as Private/unreviewed/AI-off and denied
  to a fresh AI read until person review.
- Replay, conflict, expiry, revoke-before-finish, cross-owner, invalid bytes,
  and provider-unavailable cases behave and clean up exactly.
- Catalog, aliases, `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, `llms.txt`, connection center, and stale-
  tool guidance remain synchronized.
