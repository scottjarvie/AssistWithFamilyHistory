## Why this exists

A researcher sometimes needs to share a bounded reviewed view with someone
without an account, without making it public or calling them a collaborator.

## Current truth

The product supports Private owner work and guarded Public story publication.
It has no verified unlisted-link record, reviewed content boundary, recipient
disclosure, revoke path, no-expiry policy, or separate activity history.

## Scope

- Create read-only, non-indexed, non-expiring-by-default links to an explicit
  reviewed selected-object/view boundary for appropriate stories, collections,
  source/record surfaces, or connected views.
- Say plainly that anyone with the link can view it.
- Review and filter living/sensitive/private/unreviewed material at the
  backend; do not infer deceased status from age alone.
- Provide owner preview, copy, activity, and immediate revoke controls.
- Keep Unlisted separate from Trusted collaboration and Public publication.

## Next safe action

Design the link/content boundary and threat model after the Now journey and
data-lifecycle controls are proven.

## Unknowns and access limits

No recipient model, abuse control, takedown path, or public-link storage exists.
Do not prototype with real family material.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes anonymous allow/deny fixtures, noindex headers,
review filtering, link disclosure, revoke immediacy, guessing resistance,
activity history, and mobile recipient rendering.

## Definition of done

- [ ] An owner can create, preview, copy, and immediately revoke an Unlisted view.
- [ ] Anonymous recipients see only the reviewed allowlisted boundary.
- [ ] A link never broadens through newly connected people, sources, places,
  events, notes, or stories outside the selected review boundary.
- [ ] The UI discloses anyone-with-link access and default no-expiry behavior.
- [ ] Links are read-only, non-indexed, and distinct from collaboration/publication.
- [ ] Security, privacy, PR/CI/deployment, live proof, and independent audit exist.
