## Why this exists

Family History becomes understandable when sourced events, relationships, and
places can be seen together without turning uncertain evidence into fact.

## Current truth

Person workspaces have tabular timeline, relationship, and place sections, and
the data model supports events and links. `/app/timeline` is an explicit
Coming-soon placeholder with no interactive editing, public view, or multi-person
comparison. No general pedigree/relationship visualization, map, heatmap, or
migration view is verified.

## Scope

- Provide a sourced life timeline with research-gap and story-reading modes.
- Show relationships with proposed/disputed/canonical distinctions and source links.
- Show places and migration/context only where records support them.
- Keep historical context as setting, never silent proof of personal experience.
- Use a coherent archival evidence-thread visual language across the views.

## Next safe action

Start with the read-only person timeline already described in source, then
validate whether relationship and place views share enough data/interaction to
remain one delivery route or need later split Work Orders.

## Unknowns and access limits

User priority among timeline, relationship, and map work is not proven. Map
providers, geocoding quality, ambiguous dates/places, and public publishing are
unresolved; use synthetic records.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes conflicting/partial date fixtures, provisional
relationships, ambiguous places, source drill-through, missing-context labels,
mobile/desktop rendering, and no invented locations or chronology.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Timeline entries remain tied to evidence and expose uncertainty.
- [ ] Relationship views distinguish canonical, proposed, and disputed links.
- [ ] Place/migration views do not infer unsupported personal experience.
- [ ] Research and story modes preserve the same underlying truth.
- [ ] Placeholder/internal issue labels are removed from user-facing status.
- [ ] Performance, accessibility, PR/CI/deployment, and audit proof exist.
