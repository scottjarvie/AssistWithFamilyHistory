## Why this exists

Private family records are safe only if deployed identity, owner binding, and
recovery behavior work outside source-code fixtures. Every authenticated user
journey depends on that proof.

## Current truth

Source supports Clerk sign-in, owner-scoped Convex callers, protected routes,
shadow/enforce trust modes, and two anonymous redacted public-story reads.
Signed-out production `/app` correctly redirects to Clerk at desktop and phone
widths. Full local owner-isolation contracts pass. The audit did not sign in,
inspect provider environment values, prove enforce mode, test a second owner,
or exercise sign-up, verification, sign-out, recovery, and session expiry.

## Scope

- Prove sign-up/sign-in, redirect, sign-out, expired session, recovery, and
  reauthentication with synthetic accounts.
- Verify auth requirement, anonymous-vault policy, Clerk issuer/JWT template,
  Convex deployment parity, and trust-boundary mode in the exact environment.
- Prove owner A cannot read, link, mutate, publish, migrate, or enumerate owner
  B records, and anonymous callers receive only allowed public DTOs.
- Record sparse audit receipts without tokens, private ids, or family content.

## Next safe action

Prepare an isolated preview test with two synthetic owners. Stop before durable
account creation or production writes until Scott approves that exact action.
