## Why this exists

The family rule since 1 September is that the person's OAuth sign-in and Allow
grant the ordinary toolset immediately; Settings narrows or revokes but is never
a second gate. Family History creates a pending connection with no permissions,
so a newly connected AI sees no tools until the person ticks seven permissions.

## Current truth

Signing in creates or renews an active grant with the seven ordinary scopes over
the whole workspace for 90 days (`convex/mcpGrants.ts` `resolveForRequest`).
Settings narrows or revokes; revoked and denied connections stay refused.

## Scope (subject to AWF-0052 decision 8)

- First contact creates an active grant with the ordinary read and save
  permissions over the whole workspace; publish, delete, merge, share and provider
  actions remain unissuable.
- Files not reviewed as AI-readable and living-person private notes stay
  withheld, as today.
- Settings becomes narrow/revoke; migrate pending rows; rewrite refusal text,
  `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, `/llms.txt` and the Project Philosophy.
