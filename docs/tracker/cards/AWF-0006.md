## Why this exists

The friendly product start is People and relationships. A careful import can
accelerate that first picture, but it must not become a mandatory container or
make FamilySearch look like the product itself.

## Current truth

Version 2.0.1 gives an empty signed-in workspace two truthful starts: bring in
a reviewed capture or connect a chosen AI. That repair removed the dead end,
but the philosophy's friendliest start is still missing: add one person and one
known relationship directly, privately, without requiring an import or AI
connection. The existing review-first capture flow remains an optional
accelerator with provenance and warnings.

## Scope

- Explain the private-beta purpose, storage boundary, and first useful outcome
  before or immediately after sign-in.
- Detect an empty vault and offer People and relationships as the friendly
  first step: begin with someone and a known family connection.
- Offer an authorized import—from current supported capture, an exported tree,
  research files, or a future verified path—as an accelerator that can create
  or extend the first connected picture after review, never as a required route.
- Preserve many-to-many connections so one source/document/media/place/event
  can relate to several people and context records.
- Preserve provider consent and pacing rules; do not automate FamilySearch
  browsing or handle credentials outside the user's session.
- Provide useful empty, loading, error, canceled, invalid-package, warning, and
  accepted-but-unverifiable recovery states.
- Keep obvious non-AI and no-import paths; AI setup or provider access must not
  block the first useful family picture.

## Next safe action

Execute the bounded AWF-WO-010 slice: characterize the owner-scoped people and
relationship write model, design the minimum empty-workspace entry, save one
synthetic connected pair, and prove the normal private workspace reflects it.

## Unknowns and access limits

- The empty signed-in production workspace is proven, but the direct manual
  people/relationship write path is not implemented.
- Fresh-device email verification remains a separate proof gap and does not
  block designing this owner-scoped product slice.
- No real FamilySearch person or private owner vault may be used in repository
  evidence.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence uses a synthetic, deceased fixture in an approved QA vault and must show
the signed-out entry, first authenticated view, preview, blocked and warning
states, successful merge, and safe retry behavior at phone, tablet, and desktop
widths with keyboard and console results.

## Definition of done

- [x] A new beta user can explain the private connected archive before adding data.
- [ ] An empty vault makes starting with a person and known relationship easy.
- [ ] Import is optional, review-first, provenance-preserving, and capable of
  extending the connected picture without implying direct provider access.
- [ ] Preview and warnings remain mandatory before merge.
- [ ] Empty, loading, validation, backend, and retry states are understandable and non-destructive.
- [ ] The flow has 44px phone targets, visible focus, no page overflow, and no blocking console error.
- [ ] Synthetic QA, full CI, preview/deployment, and live-environment evidence exist.

## Current / Partial / Later

- **Current:** empty-workspace guidance, reviewed capture, chosen-AI setup, and
  private owner-scoped records.
- **Partial:** people and relationships exist in the durable model and MCP, but
  not as one ordinary manual first-start flow.
- **Later:** broad tree building, provider sync, collaboration, and automated
  research; none belongs in this first slice.
