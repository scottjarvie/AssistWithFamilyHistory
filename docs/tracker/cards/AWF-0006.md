## Why this exists

The product's strongest current value starts with a careful FamilySearch
capture, but a new visitor is sent from “Enter Studio” directly to a generic
sign-in form. After sign-in, the dashboard emphasizes Story Studio and the
research queue even when a new vault has nothing to review or write about.

## Current truth

The production `/app` entry redirects to Clerk and works at desktop and phone
widths without horizontal overflow or console errors. The form does not teach
what happens after sign-in, what data will be stored, or why a first import is
the useful next step. Source contains a strong review-first import surface with
preview, blocking validation, warnings, merge recovery, and empty/backend
states, but this audit could not authenticate and prove that deployed journey.

## Scope

- Explain the private-beta purpose, storage boundary, and first useful outcome
  before or immediately after sign-in.
- Detect an empty vault and lead with the user-mediated FamilySearch extension
  capture → paste package → preview → review warnings → merge sequence.
- Preserve provider consent and pacing rules; do not automate FamilySearch
  browsing or handle credentials outside the user's session.
- Provide useful empty, loading, error, canceled, invalid-package, warning, and
  accepted-but-unverifiable recovery states.
- Keep an obvious non-AI path; AI setup must not block the first import.

## Next safe action

Design the smallest first-run state around the existing import contracts and
synthetic capture fixture. Use a normal protected software branch and do not
change auth, provider, or production data without its explicit gate.

## Unknowns and access limits

- Authenticated production onboarding was unavailable in this audit.
- Sign-up policy, email verification, MFA, account recovery, and post-sign-up
  redirect behavior were not exercised.
- No real FamilySearch person or private owner vault may be used in repository
  evidence.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence uses a synthetic, deceased fixture in an approved QA vault and must show
the signed-out entry, first authenticated view, preview, blocked and warning
states, successful merge, and safe retry behavior at phone, tablet, and desktop
widths with keyboard and console results.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A new beta user can explain what the product stores before importing.
- [ ] An empty vault points to the reviewed capture-import path, not an unusable downstream tool.
- [ ] Preview and warnings remain mandatory before merge.
- [ ] Empty, loading, validation, backend, and retry states are understandable and non-destructive.
- [ ] The flow has 44px phone targets, visible focus, no page overflow, and no blocking console error.
- [ ] Synthetic QA, full CI, preview/deployment, and live-environment evidence exist.
