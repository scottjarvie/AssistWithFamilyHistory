## Why this exists

The family ruled on 1 September that the Queue has three lifecycle states plus a
Needs-your-input flag, with archive, an item page per request, AI-added items,
and AI Suggestions for unrequested ideas. Family History still uses the retired
four states.

## Current truth

`/app/queue` works with Needs you / Working / Waiting for your AI / Done, delete
but no archive, no item page, and no AI Suggestions. The uncommitted note in
`docs/product/queue-foundation-design-handoff.md` records the mismatch.

## Scope

- Backend and UI vocabulary: Waiting on AI · AI working · Answered, Needs your
  input as a flag (including on Answered); tabs in family order with Everything
  last; archive and Show archived.
- `/queue` and `/queue/[id]` item page with the original ask, context, answers,
  partial results, linked records and history.
- Attributed AI-added items (for the person, and the AI's own follow-ups).
- AI Suggestions surface; accepting creates a Queue item.
- Project Philosophy Queue text updated to match.

## Next safe action

Map current states to the family model and write the data migration.

- 2026-10-06: Recorded from the read-only 6 October audits summarized in [the Site Guide](../../planning/site-guide.md#where-family-history-stands-6-october-2026). No application, provider or account change.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Queue uses the family states, order, flag and archive, backend and UI.

- [ ] AI Suggestions exist and accepting one creates a Queue item.
- [ ] Live on the production site.
