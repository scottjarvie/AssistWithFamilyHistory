## Why this exists

Researchers use evidence-heavy workflows on phones and desktops. The shell
must stay legible, operable, and product-native rather than becoming a shrunken
desktop table or inaccessible drawer.

## Current truth

Source has responsive layouts, an app loading skeleton, an error boundary,
semantic headings, focus styles, and a mobile drawer. Public home/sign-in checks
showed no horizontal overflow at 360px, but several visible controls measured
about 30–40px rather than the 44px Core target. The app has no verified light/
dark choice, five-destination phone bottom navigation, or authenticated
cross-route accessibility proof.

## Scope

- Provide first-class light/dark themes without erasing archival character.
- Replace or supplement the mobile drawer with at most five primary phone
  destinations and a clear route to the rest.
- Meet target size, focus, contrast, reduced-motion, landmark, status-label,
  keyboard, screen-reader, and 320px overflow requirements.
- Verify dense evidence pages use drill-down rather than page-level scrolling.

## Next safe action

Run an authenticated route matrix with synthetic data and capture failures by
shared shell versus route-specific content before changing navigation.

## Unknowns and access limits

Authenticated desktop/mobile UI, assistive technology output, long localized
content, and real dense-vault behavior were unavailable.

## Evidence

Acceptance evidence includes 320/390/768/1280/1440 screenshots and DOM checks,
keyboard/focus order, contrast, reduced motion, accessible names, screen-reader
smoke, theme persistence, and representative dense routes.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Shell and representative routes meet WCAG AA-oriented checks.
- [ ] Phone navigation has at most five primary destinations and 44px targets.
- [ ] Light/dark themes preserve product identity and readable status meaning.
- [ ] No page-level overflow or desktop-table shrinkage occurs at 320px.
- [ ] Loading, empty, error, session-expired, and focus-return states work.
- [ ] PR/CI/deployment, authenticated live proof, and independent audit exist.
