## Goal

Let a chosen AI help build a bounded, source-grounded branch for deceased
ancestors, investigate one meaningful conflict, and create a reviewable family
story packet while the product preserves claims, evidence, provenance, privacy,
history, and the person's final authority.

## Starting truth

AWF-WO-011 owns grants, tools, protected evidence bytes, connection experience,
and the remaining deployed lifecycle. AWF-WO-012 proposes the person-confirmed
conflict choice. This scenario depends on both and must not bypass living-person,
rights, source-quality, or identity-resolution gates.

## Sequence

1. Create a marked synthetic deceased-ancestor starting point with relationships,
   places, events, citations, one scanned record, and one deliberate conflict.
2. Let the AI search and read only the approved branch and evidence; distinguish
   sourced claims, interpretations, and unresolved possibilities.
3. Save new facts/citations/research notes in a coherent batch with per-item
   receipts, stable keys, source/date/provenance, and review state.
4. Produce evidence-backed alternatives for the conflict. The AI recommends;
   the person confirms which record to believe or leaves it unresolved. No
   automatic merge, overwrite, or identity collapse.
5. Build a family story packet that links every important assertion to its
   evidence and carries uncertainty forward. Publication remains a separate
   person-only action.
6. Add only missing Family-History-native record/tool/view support exposed by
   the scenario. Prove edit, archive/restore, Queue continuation, protected
   media delivery, revoke, reconnect, and exact cleanup.
